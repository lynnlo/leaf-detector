Power

  • Battery + → +12V on both L298N boards.
  • Battery − → GND on both boards and a Pi GND (6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39).
  • Common ground is mandatory — without it the inputs float and motors twitch or ignore commands.
  • Power the Pi 5 from its own USB-C supply (5 V / 5 A). Never feed the L298N +5V output into the Pi.
  • Motors connect to OUT1…OUT4, never to the logic header.

Before you power up

  • Remove ENA and ENB jumpers on board 1.
  • Remove the ENA jumper on board 2.
  • Check no 5 V wire lands on a GPIO.
  • Wheel spins the wrong way? Swap that motor's two output wires — not the GPIO pins.
